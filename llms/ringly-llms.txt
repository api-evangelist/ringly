# Ringly

> Ringly (ringly.io) is AI-powered phone support built for Shopify e-commerce brands. Its AI voice agent "Seth" answers customer service calls 24/7 — WISMO (where-is-my-order), returns, product, inventory, and policy questions — and takes real actions via Shopify order/catalog data and helpdesk/tracking tools (Gorgias, TrackingMore). Delivered as a hosted SaaS and a Shopify app. As of this profile, Ringly publishes no public developer API, SDK, or webhook surface.

## About

- Company: Ringly
- Category: Conversational AI, AI voice agent, e-commerce customer support
- Delivery: Hosted SaaS + Shopify app
- Integrations: Shopify (primary), Gorgias (helpdesk), TrackingMore (order tracking)
- Positioning: 65% call-resolution guarantee within 90 days; plans priced by included call minutes
- Backed by: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)
